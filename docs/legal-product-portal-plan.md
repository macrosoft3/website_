# Legal Product Portal Plan

> This document is an engineering compliance checklist, not legal advice.
> Before public release, paid distribution, user-data collection, or production operation, confirm the final position with a qualified professional.


## Scope

The Ether LLC website is the right public home for cross-product legal, privacy, support, and company information.

## Required Improvements

- Add a product legal index linking to GratefulMoments, MindVault, LaunchGrid, Tsumugi, UkiyoLens, and future apps.
- Clarify that listed business purposes are corporate purposes and not necessarily services currently provided.
- Add a non-investment-advice notice to Ethereum-related pages where appropriate.
- Keep Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy updated when analytics, forms, newsletters, SDKs, or external transmission mechanisms are added.
- Keep inquiry handling aligned with the published Privacy Policy.
